NYUMBALINK FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about finding and listing property on NyumbaLink.

What is NyumbaLink?

NyumbaLink is a property platform that helps people find affordable houses and land directly in their preferred county or town.

Can I search for houses by town?

Yes. Choose a county first, then select an available town or area to narrow your search.

Can I search for land too?

Yes. Select “Buy land” in the search panel and choose the property type that fits your needs.

Do I have to pay an agent to find a property?

NyumbaLink is designed to help seekers connect directly with landlords and property sellers, reducing unnecessary agent fees.

How do I post a property?

Click “Post property” or “Landlord sign up”, enter the property details, and continue through the listing process.

How much does it cost to list a property?

The current starter listing flow is free. Any premium listing options can be added later as the platform grows.

How do I contact a landlord?

Open a property listing and use the contact details provided by the landlord or seller.

Can I browse properties without an account?

Yes. Property browsing and searching can be available without signing in. An account is useful when you want to list or manage properties.

Which areas does NyumbaLink cover?

NyumbaLink is designed for properties across Kenya, with searches organized by county, town and area.

Is NyumbaLink available on phones?

Yes. The website is responsive and is designed to work comfortably on phones, tablets and laptop screens.
